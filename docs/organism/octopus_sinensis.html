East Asian common octopus (Octopus sinensis)

The closest species with at least 100 curator-checked (Swiss-Prot) proteins is Californiconus californicus; the two lineages split 535 million years ago (Mya). In human proteins searched against a species that far away (sea squirt (Ciona), 550 Mya), the three tools with the best Fmax (the balance of finding domains and being right) were MMseqs2; MMseqs2, three rounds; phmmer, and MMseqs2 found 40% of the known Pfam domains. In the measured organism closest to this one, nematode (Caenorhabditis elegans), 45% of proteins had no hit from any sequence tool, so expect a share like that to stay without a label.

downloads this species' proteins and Swiss-Prot, runs nf-core/proteinannotator 1.1.0, then the three tools; needs a Unix computer with curl, Nextflow and Docker
# East Asian common octopus (Octopus sinensis)
# UniProt reference proteome UP000515154, 30,311 proteins
curl -L -o proteome.fasta.gz 'https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/stream?query=proteome%3AUP000515154&format=fasta&compressed=true'
# All reviewed Swiss-Prot proteins, the source of the labels (they include those of the nearest reviewed relative, Californiconus californicus).
# Swiss-Prot has about 575,000 proteins; the board measured cost against one relative's proteome of about 20,000, so expect longer runs.
curl -L -o swissprot.fasta.gz 'https://ftp.uniprot.org/pub/databases/uniprot/current_release/knowledgebase/complete/uniprot_sprot.fasta.gz'
# nf-core/proteinannotator 1.1.0: Pfam, FunFam and InterProScan on your proteome.
# Version 1.1.0 has no option to run MMseqs2; MMseqs2, three rounds; phmmer; their commands follow.
printf 'id,fasta\noctopus_sinensis,proteome.fasta.gz\n' > samplesheet.csv
nextflow run nf-core/proteinannotator -r 1.1.0 -profile docker --input samplesheet.csv --outdir results

# -E 10.0 keeps every hit, as on the board. The board's numbers count calls above the score cut-off
# that gives each tool its best Fmax; filter the output before you take labels from it.

# MMseqs2, sensitivity 7, one round (quay.io/biocontainers/mmseqs2:18.8cc5c--hd6d6fdc_0 (sha256:3503bfe576d560e550df2872af86a1ad1bcc1c06cfb7caadd3e7a95649f5f0ef))
mmseqs createdb proteome.fasta.gz q_db
mmseqs createdb swissprot.fasta.gz t_db
mmseqs search q_db t_db result_s7 tmp_s7 --threads 16 -s 7 --max-seqs 1000 -e 10.0
mmseqs convertalis q_db t_db result_s7 mmseqs2_s7.m8

# MMseqs2, sensitivity 7, three rounds (quay.io/biocontainers/mmseqs2:18.8cc5c--hd6d6fdc_0 (sha256:3503bfe576d560e550df2872af86a1ad1bcc1c06cfb7caadd3e7a95649f5f0ef))
mmseqs search q_db t_db result_s7_n3 tmp_s7_n3 --threads 16 -s 7 --num-iterations 3 --max-seqs 1000 -e 10.0
mmseqs convertalis q_db t_db result_s7_n3 mmseqs2_s7_3rounds.m8

# phmmer (quay.io/biocontainers/hmmer:3.4--hb6cb901_4 (sha256:7a2b317b8d2fd3650b4924a8482cddeb940d4a0746c6a1501ff03ac1b7439e0c))
gunzip -kf proteome.fasta.gz swissprot.fasta.gz
phmmer --domtblout phmmer.domtbl --noali -E 10.0 --cpu 8 proteome.fasta swissprot.fasta > /dev/null

Three tools, in the order to run them

No AlphaFold models counted for this species, so the ranking uses all domains at this distance.

How this was measured: 998 human proteins were searched against the proteome of sea squirt (Ciona) (Ciona intestinalis), whose lineage split from human 550 Mya. Of the 15 relatives measured, its split age is the closest to this species' 535 Mya. Split age is a stand-in for how much proteins have changed; lineages change at different speeds. Counted: 2,435 Pfam domains in human proteins of any AlphaFold confidence. Tools are ranked by Fmax, the best balance of finding domains (recall) and being right (precision), 1 being perfect; the three differ by 0.002 in Fmax, a gap this one set of proteins cannot settle, so if cost matters pick the cheapest of them.
