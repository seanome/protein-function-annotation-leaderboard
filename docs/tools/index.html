    phmmer

    Amino-acid letters, one query protein against each protein of the relative, scored with a substitution matrix the way BLAST does but with HMMER's model of insertions and deletions.

    1 setting · 15 relatives · 3 truth sets
  • aligns letterssequence onlypermissive licence

    MMseqs2

    Amino-acid letters: it first keeps target proteins that share similar short words with the query, then aligns only those, which makes it much faster than aligning every pair.

    1 setting · 15 relatives · 3 truth sets
  • family profilessequence onlypermissive licence

    jackhmmer

    A profile of a protein family: it searches with one protein, builds a profile from the hits, and searches again with that profile, three rounds in all.

    1 setting · 15 relatives · 3 truth sets
  • family profilessequence onlypermissive licence

    MMseqs2, three rounds

    A profile of a protein family, built by MMseqs2 from its own hits over three rounds of search, as jackhmmer does.

    1 setting · 15 relatives · 3 truth sets
  • family profilessequence onlyGPL-3.0

    HH-suite (hhsearch)

    Two profiles against each other.

    1 setting · 15 relatives · 3 truth sets

    ProstT5 + Foldseek

    Shape letters (3Di) predicted from the amino-acid sequence by the ProstT5 protein language model, then searched with Foldseek, so no AlphaFold model is needed.

    1 setting · 15 relatives · 3 truth sets

    kmerseek

    Short words of k letters (k-mers) shared between two proteins.

    15 settings · 15 relatives · 3 truth sets · 1 alive, 2 retired, 3 untested claims

    hmmscan with Pfam

    Each query protein against the curated profile models of every Pfam family, without searching any relative.
