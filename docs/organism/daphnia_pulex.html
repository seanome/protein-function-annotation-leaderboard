common water flea (Daphnia pulex)

The closest species with at least 100 curator-checked (Swiss-Prot) proteins is fruit fly (Drosophila melanogaster); the two lineages split 512 million years ago (Mya). In human proteins searched against a species that far away (sea squirt (Ciona), 550 Mya), the three tools with the best Fmax (the balance of finding domains and being right) were MMseqs2, three rounds; HH-suite (hhsearch); MMseqs2, and MMseqs2, three rounds found 42% of the known Pfam domains. In the measured organism closest to this one, nematode (Caenorhabditis elegans), 45% of proteins had no hit from any sequence tool, so expect a share like that to stay without a label.

downloads this species' proteins and Swiss-Prot, runs nf-core/proteinannotator 1.1.0, then the three tools; needs a Unix computer with curl, Nextflow and Docker
# common water flea (Daphnia pulex)
# UniProt reference proteome UP000000305, 30,137 proteins
curl -L -o proteome.fasta.gz 'https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/stream?query=proteome%3AUP000000305&format=fasta&compressed=true'
# All reviewed Swiss-Prot proteins, the source of the labels (they include those of the nearest reviewed relative, Drosophila melanogaster).
# Swiss-Prot has about 575,000 proteins; the board measured cost against one relative's proteome of about 20,000, so expect longer runs.
curl -L -o swissprot.fasta.gz 'https://ftp.uniprot.org/pub/databases/uniprot/current_release/knowledgebase/complete/uniprot_sprot.fasta.gz'
# nf-core/proteinannotator 1.1.0: Pfam, FunFam and InterProScan on your proteome.
# Version 1.1.0 has no option to run MMseqs2, three rounds; HH-suite (hhsearch); MMseqs2; their commands follow.
printf 'id,fasta\ndaphnia_pulex,proteome.fasta.gz\n' > samplesheet.csv
nextflow run nf-core/proteinannotator -r 1.1.0 -profile docker --input samplesheet.csv --outdir results

# -E 10.0 keeps every hit, as on the board. The board's numbers count calls above the score cut-off
# that gives each tool its best Fmax; filter the output before you take labels from it.

mmseqs convertalis q_db t_db result_s7_n3 mmseqs2_s7_3rounds.m8

Three tools, in the order to run them

How this was measured: 998 human proteins were searched against the proteome of sea squirt (Ciona) (Ciona intestinalis), whose lineage split from human 550 Mya. Of the 15 relatives measured, its split age is the closest to this species' 512 Mya. Split age is a stand-in for how much proteins have changed; lineages change at different speeds. Counted: 748 Pfam domains in human proteins whose mean AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT, 0–100; AlphaFold DB calls 70 to 90 confident and above 90 very high) is 50-70. Tools are ranked by Fmax, the best balance of finding domains (recall) and being right (precision), 1 being perfect; the three differ by 0.008 in Fmax, a gap this one set of proteins cannot settle, so if cost matters pick the cheapest of them.
