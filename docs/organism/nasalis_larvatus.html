proboscis monkey (Nasalis larvatus)

The closest species with at least 100 curator-checked (Swiss-Prot) proteins is crab-eating macaque (Macaca fascicularis); the two lineages split 18 million years ago (Mya). In human proteins searched against a species that far away (chimpanzee, 6 Mya), the three tools with the best Fmax (the balance of finding domains and being right) were kmerseek; phmmer; HH-suite (hhsearch), and kmerseek found 39% of the known Pfam domains. In the measured organism closest to this one, human (Homo sapiens), 15% of proteins had no hit from any sequence tool, so expect a share like that to stay without a label.

downloads this species' proteins and Swiss-Prot, runs nf-core/proteinannotator 1.1.0, then the three tools; needs a Unix computer with curl, Nextflow and Docker
# proboscis monkey (Nasalis larvatus)
# UniProt reference proteome UP001715597, 22,641 proteins
curl -L -o proteome.fasta.gz 'https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/stream?query=proteome%3AUP001715597&format=fasta&compressed=true'
# All reviewed Swiss-Prot proteins, the source of the labels (they include those of the nearest reviewed relative, Macaca fascicularis).
# Swiss-Prot has about 575,000 proteins; the board measured cost against one relative's proteome of about 20,000, so expect longer runs.
curl -L -o swissprot.fasta.gz 'https://ftp.uniprot.org/pub/databases/uniprot/current_release/knowledgebase/complete/uniprot_sprot.fasta.gz'
# nf-core/proteinannotator 1.1.0: Pfam, FunFam and InterProScan on your proteome.
# Version 1.1.0 has no option to run kmerseek; phmmer; HH-suite (hhsearch); their commands follow.
printf 'id,fasta\nnasalis_larvatus,proteome.fasta.gz\n' > samplesheet.csv
nextflow run nf-core/proteinannotator -r 1.1.0 -profile docker --input samplesheet.csv --outdir results

Three tools, in the order to run them

No AlphaFold models counted for this species, so the ranking uses all domains at this distance.

How this was measured: 998 human proteins were searched against the proteome of chimpanzee (Pan troglodytes), whose lineage split from human 6 Mya. Of the 15 relatives measured, its split age is the closest to this species' 18 Mya. Split age is a stand-in for how much proteins have changed; lineages change at different speeds. Counted: 2,435 Pfam domains in human proteins of any AlphaFold confidence. Tools are ranked by Fmax, the best balance of finding domains (recall) and being right (precision), 1 being perfect; the three differ by 0.132 in Fmax, a gap this one set of proteins cannot settle, so if cost matters pick the cheapest of them.

What no tool on this board will find for you

15.1% of 20,600 proteins of human (Homo sapiens) had no hit from any sequence tool, even at a loose E-value below 1 (the E-value is the number of hits that size expected by chance). They were searched against Swiss-Prot with the organism's own group removed; we call these proteins dark. Of the five organisms measured, this one is closest to this species: their lineages meet at Catarrhini.
