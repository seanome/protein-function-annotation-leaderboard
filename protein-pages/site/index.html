One page per protein of your proteome. On each page, every tool's hits are drawn on the same ruler of residue positions. Each program counts once, even when it was run twice (MMseqs2 one round and three rounds; Foldseek on predicted structures and ProstT5 + Foldseek), so there are 8 programs for 10 rows. A hit counts when its E-value is at most 10 (hhsearch: also Prob at least 50). "Agree" means two hits from different programs overlap by at least half of the shorter one and name the same Pfam family or InterPro entry. The small picture is the "programs with a hit" line of each protein page: its height at each point is the number of programs (0 to 8) with a hit over that part of the protein, squeezed to the same 60-pixel width for every protein whatever its length. "Best E-value, any tool" is the lowest E-value any tool printed (each tool computes its own, so they are not on one scale); 0 means the tool printed 0.

The search looks at every hit counted above: the name, gene and species of each Swiss-Prot protein a tool matched, the Pfam families on the matched part of it, and the Pfam families hmmscan or InterProScan found on your protein. Every word typed must appear; the last column shows what matched and which programs' hits carry it.
