Reduced alphabets

For anyone building a prefilter on a reduced amino acid alphabet: three measures for each of kmerseek's 19 alphabets. How much evidence of relatedness one aligned position keeps. How far above chance the classes of aligned residues agree. Which seed lengths k can reach homologs.

Data from 16 Oct 2026, commit 98e9bccb (source)

This page measures alphabets with the alignment given. Each pair of proteins was aligned already, by Pfam's curators or by structure; the page asks how much of that alignment each alphabet's classes keep. It does not rank search tools, and it does not say which alphabet finds the most homologs in a search, because a search has to find the alignment first. Use it to choose which alphabets and seed lengths are worth testing in a search, then test them on the leaderboard.

In the fair test on Pfam seed pairs at 20% identity or less, three alphabets keep more evidence per aligned position than the 20 amino acids, with the 95% interval above 1 (sdm12, hsdm17, mmseqs12). sdm12 keeps 1.032 times as much (95% interval 1.027–1.038). Every two-class alphabet keeps less, with its interval below 1; the best of them, hp_thomas_dill2, keeps 0.79 times as much. n = 37,573 pair-halves from 10,921 Pfam families.

How to read each column

Classes. The squares in one box are residues the alphabet puts in one class, so a seed cannot tell them apart. In the seven hydrophobic/polar (H/P) alphabets, a box is amber for hydrophobic, blue for polar, grey for a cysteine class of its own. The classes are kmerseek's, copied into the analysis repository; the H/P letters are PR 116's.

Evidence per aligned position (notebook 291, PR 99). For one aligned pair, an aligned position is a column where neither protein has a gap. Each residue is replaced by its class. Agreement a is the share of positions where the two classes are the same; chance c is the share expected from the two proteins' class make-up alone. The evidence in bits is a·log₂(a/c) + (1−a)·log₂((1−a)/(1−c)): 0 when the pair agrees no more than chance, higher when one aligned position says more about whether the proteins are related. The table shows each alphabet's mean evidence divided by the 20 amino acids' on the same pairs: right of the dashed line keeps more. The 95% interval comes from resampling Pfam families, or the query domain's SCOPe fold, 2,000 times. Fair test: pairs are put in the 20% or less group by the identity of half of their positions (odd or even) and measured on the other half, then the halves are swapped. Picking pairs by the same positions that are measured favours every alphabet that merges letters (the biased view is in the full table below).

Kappa (notebook 230, PR 100). Agreement corrected for chance: (a − c) / (1 − c), the mean over Pfam seed pairs in each identity bin. 0 means no better than a shuffled partner, 1 means every aligned position agrees. Identity is the 20 amino acid identity over aligned positions. A higher kappa for a smaller alphabet does not mean it finds more homologs: merging letters also counts the common swaps between homologs (I and V, D and E) as matches.

Usable k (notebook 230, ranges from notebook 274, PR 101). An exact seed of length k can find a pair only if the pair shares an unbroken run of k aligned positions in the same class. A seed length is usable when it is inside notebook 274's range: no more than 100 human proteins share a seed by chance, and up to one chance match in Swiss-Prot. It must also reach pairs: at least 5% of the Pfam seed pairs in that identity bin share such a run. The grey line is notebook 274's range; the bar is the usable part of it.

Where the numbers come from

All tables are in results/leaderboard/alphabets/ of seanome/2024-kmerseek-analysis at commit 98e9bccb, written by make leaderboard-alphabets. Evidence values are notebook 291's stored table; the build adds the pair counts and checks that its own mean evidence equals the notebook's. Kappa and usable k are computed with notebook 230's code from its per-pair table, because the notebook prints them but does not store them. Data: Pfam-A 38.2 seed alignments (pairs with at least 50 aligned positions) and SCOPe 2.08 domains at 40% identity aligned by structure with USalign (TM-score at least 0.5).
