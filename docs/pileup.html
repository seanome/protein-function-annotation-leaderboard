Region pile-up

Where each tool's calls fall around a known domain. Every row is one of the 2,435 Pfam domains on the 998 human query proteins. Every panel is one tool and setting searching the zebrafish proteome (430 Mya), and every panel has the same rows. A residue counts as covered when the tool made a call of that domain's own Pfam family over it, at any score the tool reported (E-value cutoff 10).

Built the way deepTools draws a profile around genes (computeMatrix scale-regions, then plotHeatmap and plotProfile), on residues instead of bases. Each row holds 100 residues before the domain's start, then the domain stretched or squeezed into 100 equal bins, then 100 residues after its end. Each bin shows the residue at its centre. The median domain is 89 aa, so a bin is about 0.9 aa.
