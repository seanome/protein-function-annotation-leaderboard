Soboliphyme baturini

The closest species with at least 100 curator-checked (Swiss-Prot) proteins is Caenorhabditis elegans; the two lineages split 468 million years ago (Mya). In human proteins searched against a species that far away (zebrafish, 435 Mya), the three tools with the best Fmax (the balance of finding domains and being right) were phmmer; MMseqs2; MMseqs2, three rounds, and phmmer found 56% of the known Pfam domains. In the measured organism closest to this one, nematode (Caenorhabditis elegans), 45% of proteins had no hit from any sequence tool, so expect a share like that to stay without a label.

downloads this species' proteins and Swiss-Prot, runs nf-core/proteinannotator 1.1.0, then the three tools; needs a Unix computer with curl, Nextflow and Docker
# Soboliphyme baturini
# UniProt reference proteome UP000270296, 13,153 proteins
curl -L -o proteome.fasta.gz 'https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/stream?query=proteome%3AUP000270296&format=fasta&compressed=true'
# All reviewed Swiss-Prot proteins, the source of the labels (they include those of the nearest reviewed relative, Caenorhabditis elegans).
# Swiss-Prot has about 575,000 proteins; the board measured cost against one relative's proteome of about 20,000, so expect longer runs.
curl -L -o swissprot.fasta.gz 'https://ftp.uniprot.org/pub/databases/uniprot/current_release/knowledgebase/complete/uniprot_sprot.fasta.gz'
# nf-core/proteinannotator 1.1.0: Pfam, FunFam and InterProScan on your proteome.
# Version 1.1.0 has no option to run phmmer; MMseqs2; MMseqs2, three rounds; their commands follow.
printf 'id,fasta\nsoboliphyme_baturini,proteome.fasta.gz\n' > samplesheet.csv
nextflow run nf-core/proteinannotator -r 1.1.0 -profile docker --input samplesheet.csv --outdir results

# -E 10.0 keeps every hit, as on the board. The board's numbers count calls above the score cut-off
# that gives each tool its best Fmax; filter the output before you take labels from it.

# phmmer (quay.io/biocontainers/hmmer:3.4--hb6cb901_4 (sha256:7a2b317b8d2fd3650b4924a8482cddeb940d4a0746c6a1501ff03ac1b7439e0c))
gunzip -kf proteome.fasta.gz swissprot.fasta.gz
phmmer --domtblout phmmer.domtbl --noali -E 10.0 --cpu 8 proteome.fasta swissprot.fasta > /dev/null

# MMseqs2, sensitivity 7, one round (quay.io/biocontainers/mmseqs2:18.8cc5c--hd6d6fdc_0 (sha256:3503bfe576d560e550df2872af86a1ad1bcc1c06cfb7caadd3e7a95649f5f0ef))
mmseqs createdb proteome.fasta.gz q_db
mmseqs createdb swissprot.fasta.gz t_db
mmseqs search q_db t_db result_s7 tmp_s7 --threads 16 -s 7 --max-seqs 1000 -e 10.0
mmseqs convertalis q_db t_db result_s7 mmseqs2_s7.m8

# MMseqs2, sensitivity 7, three rounds (quay.io/biocontainers/mmseqs2:18.8cc5c--hd6d6fdc_0 (sha256:3503bfe576d560e550df2872af86a1ad1bcc1c06cfb7caadd3e7a95649f5f0ef))
mmseqs search q_db t_db result_s7_n3 tmp_s7_n3 --threads 16 -s 7 --num-iterations 3 --max-seqs 1000 -e 10.0
mmseqs convertalis q_db t_db result_s7_n3 mmseqs2_s7_3rounds.m8

Three tools, in the order to run them

How this was measured: 998 human proteins were searched against the proteome of zebrafish (Danio rerio), whose lineage split from human 435 Mya. Of the 14 relatives measured that have a TimeTree age, its split age is the closest to this species' 468 Mya. Split age is a stand-in for how much proteins have changed; lineages change at different speeds. Counted: 748 Pfam domains in human proteins whose mean AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT, 0–100; AlphaFold DB calls 70 to 90 confident and above 90 very high) is 50-70. Tools are ranked by Fmax, the best balance of finding domains (recall) and being right (precision), 1 being perfect; the three differ by 0.017 in Fmax, a gap this one set of proteins cannot settle, so if cost matters pick the cheapest of them.
